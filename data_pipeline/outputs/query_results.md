## Q5 - BETWEEN - mid-priced books (GBP 20-40) rated 4 or 5

```sql
SELECT title, price_gbp, rating
        FROM books
        WHERE price_gbp BETWEEN 20.0 AND 40.0
```

## Q6 - JOIN (the equivalence query) - top-rated books per category

```sql
SELECT c.category_name,
               b.title,
               b.rating,
               b.price_gbp,
               b.price_inr
        FROM books AS b
        INNER JOIN categories AS c
                ON c.category_id = b.category_id
```
